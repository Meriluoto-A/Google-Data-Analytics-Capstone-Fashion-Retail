## CAPSTONE PROJECT: Fashion Retail

Author: Anne M
Date: 27 August 2026

This Capstone Project for the Google Data Analytics Certificate course follows the six step data analysis process: 

1.	Ask – define the business problem
2.	Prepare – gather data
3.	Process – clean the data
4.	Analyse – analyse to find the insights
5.	Share – share the results
6.	Act – turn insights into decisions

## ASK
BUSINESS TASK:  
1.	Top-performing products & brands: Which products and brands generate the highest sales, revenue, and customer satisfaction?
2.	Seasonal variation: How do sales vary across seasons, and which product categories experience the strongest seasonal demand shifts?
3.	Product returns: Which products have the highest return rates, what are the most common return reasons?
4.	Markdown effectiveness: How does markdown percentage influence sales volume?

STAKEHOLDERS: 
•	Inventory and Supply Chain: Needs seasonal demand forecasts and stock optimization insights
•	Marketing Team: Using top-performing brands and seasonal peaks for targeted campaigns.
•	Management: Requires high-level performance insights, profitability trends, and strategic recommendations.
